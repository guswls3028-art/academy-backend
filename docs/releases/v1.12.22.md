## Scope and owning contract

The operator command `check_state_integrity --tenant <exact-id> --dry-run`
compares existing session exam-pass projections with the canonical representative
results, explicit targets, and lecture policy. It preserves manual exceptions,
defers unsettled asynchronous sources, and fails closed on incomplete inspection.
Dry-run writes neither business data nor audit receipts and sends nothing.

Non-dry execution has separate append-only operational receipts and delivery
state, without changing product rows. Unknown delivery is not blindly retried.
The full current contract, permissions, failure modes, limits, and tests are in
[state-integrity-monitor.md](../operations/state-integrity-monitor.md).
