# V1.11.26 Release Notes

**Status:** Production-deployed / GO for bank-transfer collection

**Release date:** 2026-07-28 KST

**Scope:** Immediate no-PG bank-transfer collection, tenant-safe deposit
review, and evidence-backed electronic tax-invoice operations

## 1. Decision

GO for collecting HakwonPlus subscription fees by bank transfer without
activating Toss automatic card billing.

Academy owners can switch a payable subscription invoice to bank transfer,
see the exact operating account and invoice amounts, submit the depositor and
transfer time, and optionally provide the buyer business profile required for
an electronic tax invoice. Platform operators can review the actual transfer,
confirm or reject it, and record the National Tax Service approval number
after issuing the tax invoice.

Toss automatic billing remains disabled. No synthetic payment, transfer
notice, tax invoice, or subscription extension was created during production
verification.

## 2. Collection and Tax-Invoice Workflow

- The server exposes the collection account only when the four-part bank
  configuration is complete and fails closed in production otherwise.
- Switching to bank transfer changes eligible scheduled card invoices to the
  manual collection path; it does not mark an invoice paid.
- The submitted amount is always copied from the locked server invoice, never
  trusted from the browser.
- A transfer notice moves through `SUBMITTED -> CONFIRMED | REJECTED`.
- Confirmation atomically records the payment transaction, marks the invoice
  paid, and renews the subscription once.
- A requested tax invoice moves from `REQUESTED` to `READY` only after payment
  confirmation and to `ISSUED` only after a 24-digit NTS approval number is
  recorded.
- Buyer business data is snapshotted with the request so later profile edits
  do not rewrite issuance evidence.

## 3. Safety and Compatibility

- Program, invoice, and notice locks close the card-charge/bank-confirmation
  race when a billing mode changes during a payment attempt.
- A notice cannot skip an earlier unpaid manual invoice.
- The generic manual-paid endpoint cannot bypass the transfer-review
  workflow.
- Cross-tenant review requires the canonical platform-admin permission, and a
  notice derives its tenant from its invoice rather than storing a second
  independently editable tenant reference.
- Deposit and issuance times are bounded; rejected notices require a reason;
  administrative state fields are read-only.
- Repeating the same submitted notice is idempotent.
- NTS approval numbers are normalized to exactly 24 digits and are unique
  when present at both application and database layers.
- Existing card billing APIs remain available, but production Toss automatic
  billing is still explicitly off.

## 4. User and Operator Experience

- Academy settings now present a restrained bank-transfer payment slip with
  bank, account number, account holder, copy control, invoice supply amount,
  VAT, total, due date, and submission status.
- Card registration controls disappear after a real tenant switches to manual
  bank transfer, preventing a contradictory payment-mode UI.
- Platform billing includes a paginated, polling actionable queue for deposit
  confirmation, rejection, buyer tax data, and NTS approval recording.
- Loading and API failures are explicit and retryable; form state is not
  overwritten by focus refetches.
- Status updates use accessible live regions and busy states.

## 6. Audit Closure

The release was reviewed through all six version-seal lenses.

- Backend domain: atomic confirmation, idempotency, invoice ordering, amount
  drift, and card/manual mode races were closed.
- Tenant isolation: canonical platform-admin authorization, host rejection,
  audit attribution, and invoice-derived tenancy were verified.
- Frontend integration: API types, query-key prefixes, polling, pagination,
  cache invalidation, and failure rendering were corrected.
- Destructive and boundary behavior: generic paid-state bypass, editable
  admin state, future timestamps, duplicate approval numbers, and repeated
  submission were fenced.
- UX and accessibility: payment-mode wording, contradictory card controls,
  focus-safe forms, clipboard failure, busy states, and live feedback were
  reviewed.
- Security: server-owned amounts, no browser secret exposure, strict approval
  format, and fail-closed production configuration were verified.

Critical findings: 0. High findings: 0 after remediation.
