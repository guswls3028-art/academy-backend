# V1.11.30 Release Notes

**Status:** Production-deployed / GO for staff operations

**Release date:** 2026-07-28 KST

**Scope:** Tenant-safe staff lifecycle, intuitive attendance and reimbursement
operations, immutable settlement evidence, and Korean payroll-document boundaries

## 1. Decision

GO for academy owners and administrators to manage teachers and assistants
through the staff workspace.

The release closes the authorization, concurrency, historical-data, and
operator-clarity gaps found in the staff lifecycle, clock-in/out, expense
review, month close, and settlement export paths. It deliberately keeps the
generated PDF as an internal attendance and reimbursement reference rather
than presenting it as a statutory wage statement.

## 2. Owner, Administrator, and Staff Workflow

- Owners and administrators can create, edit, deactivate, reactivate, and
  safely offboard staff without erasing attendance or settlement history.
- Initial passwords require a first-login change and password resets use the
  same locked role boundary as staff lifecycle changes.
- Each staff member sees only active assigned work types. A single assignment
  can be used directly; multiple assignments require an explicit work-type
  choice with the rate shown.
- Clock-in freezes the resolved hourly rate. Later rate edits and recalculation
  do not silently rewrite historical pay evidence.
- Clock-out, break, and meal inputs reject impossible time ranges, including a
  break that consumes the full shift.
- Employee-paid costs are named and handled as staff prepaid reimbursements.
  Submitted items begin pending; approved and rejected decisions are immutable.
- Hourly month close remains automatic where supported. Legacy monthly-salary
  staff are shown as manual verification and cannot be auto-closed.
- Settlement PDF and Excel exports use a fixed snapshot set, frozen staff
  names, exact server filters, and activity in the selected month, including
  staff who were later deactivated.

## 3. Safety, Isolation, and Auditability

- Staff-payroll authorization now has one canonical permission and fails
  closed on missing or inconsistent tenant membership.
- Owner/admin protections, teacher write restrictions, and cross-tenant
  rejection are enforced server-side rather than relying on hidden controls.
- Staff, work-type assignment, password, work-record, expense-review, and
  snapshot operations use transactional row locks where races could otherwise
  lose updates or create contradictory state.
- The unique-open-work-record invariant and all clock-in eligibility checks
  are revalidated under lock.
- A staff record with history is offboarded rather than hard-deleted. Hard
  delete remains available only when no operational history exists.
- Legacy name/phone matching fails closed when more than one staff row matches.
- Snapshot export workers verify the exact sorted snapshot IDs and revisions
  selected by the request, closing the selection/export timing gap.
- Staff lifecycle, password, work-type, rate, work-record, expense-decision,
  and month-close mutations write operator audit records.

## 4. User Experience

- The administrator workspace is responsive at common laptop widths and keeps
  the roster, detail, attendance, reimbursement, settlement, and settings
  hierarchy readable without hiding critical state.
- Permission and month-lock checks fail closed with explicit retry controls;
  a transient detail fetch cannot expose editable controls.
- Offboarding, permanent deletion, reactivation, password reset, and export
  actions use distinct language and consequences.
- Staff and teacher forms reset between people, edits, and months so stale
  values cannot leak into the next operation.
- Paginated API results are normalized consistently, with bounded 500-row
  requests for operator tables and exports.
- Payroll-facing copy now says internal settlement reference, attendance
  amount, and prepaid reimbursement. It does not imply statutory payslip,
  withholding, net-pay, or a hard-coded 3.3 percent deduction.

## 5. Korean Business and Legal Boundary

- The PDF is titled `근태·경비 정산 참고서` and states that it is not a
  statutory wage statement.
- It does not claim to calculate overtime, night, holiday premiums, statutory
  deductions, or net pay when those inputs and rules are not modeled.
- Existing monthly-salary rows require manual review rather than a fabricated
  hourly conversion.
- Historical staff and settlement evidence is preserved and names are frozen
  on new snapshots.
- A complete Korean payroll engine and statutory wage-statement generator
  remain a separate scoped capability because they require employment-period,
  fixed/variable wage-component, premium-pay, deduction, and policy inputs
  that this staff module does not yet own.

## 7. Audit Closure

The release was reviewed through all six version-seal lenses.

- Backend domain: lifecycle state, clock-in/out invariants, wage freezing,
  expense decisions, month close, and fixed-set exports were hardened.

  rejected-expense mutation, stale export selection, and monthly auto-close

  one-time-password handling, action wording, and settlement terminology were
  reviewed.

  locks, immutable decisions, exact export revisions, and operator audit logs
  were verified.
