# V1.11.11 Release Notes

**Status:** Production-deployed / GO

**Release date:** 2026-07-26 KST

**Scope:** KRW 198,000 standard price, August-only KRW 159,000 lifetime
discount, single unrestricted plan, and full production closure

## 1. Decision

GO. HakwonPlus now presents one unrestricted plan with every feature included.
The standard monthly price is KRW 180,000 supply plus KRW 18,000 VAT, for a
KRW 198,000 total.

Programs created from 2026-08-01 through 2026-08-31 KST receive the August
special price of KRW 145,000 supply plus the fixed KRW 14,000 tax amount, for a
KRW 159,000 total. That cohort retains KRW 159,000 for life even after a future
standard-price increase.

## 2. Delivered Behavior

- The public pricing surface shows one plan and makes `all features included`
  explicit; there are no feature tiers or disabled capabilities.
- The normal KRW 198,000 price is shown alongside the August KRW 159,000
  special price, the KRW 39,000 monthly savings, and the lifetime
  no-increase guarantee.
- Hero, pricing, navigation, FAQ, guide, metadata, server-rendered social
  description, and structured offer data use the same pricing narrative.
- The structured offer remains KRW 159,000 through 2026-08-31 and states both
  the standard and August totals.
- New non-August contracts use the KRW 180,000 supply price and ten-percent
  VAT. New August contracts use the guaranteed KRW 145,000 supply and fixed
  KRW 14,000 tax snapshot.
- Existing pre-policy contracts retain their current snapshots. The release
  does not silently reprice any program, invoice, billing key, or payment.
- Cloudflare Pages routing now forwards `/promo/*` image assets instead of
  treating their extensions as unknown application routes.

## 3. Compatibility and Data Safety

- No schema or data migration was required.
- August lifetime eligibility is determined from the program creation time in
  KST and is normalized on later saves.
- Standard, August-guaranteed, and legacy contracts remain distinguishable in
  subscription and invoice responses.
- Mixed-contract aggregate responses do not publish a misleading common VAT
  rate; each program retains its own supply, tax, total, and guarantee facts.
- Anonymous tenant bootstrap responses continue to exclude price and
  subscription contract fields.

## 4. Six-Lens Release Review
