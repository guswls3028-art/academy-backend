# V1.11.6 Release Notes

**Status:** Production-deployed / GO

**Release date:** 2026-07-26 KST

**Scope:** August 2026 signup cohort lifetime guarantee for the unrestricted
KRW 159,000 monthly plan

## 1. Decision

GO. Programs created from 2026-08-01 through 2026-08-31 in the Korean time
zone receive a lifetime contract price of KRW 145,000 supply, KRW 14,000 VAT,
and KRW 159,000 total. Later list-price increases for new signups must not
change this cohort.

The offer contains the same unrestricted `all` plan as the standard product.
No feature, student-count, or account-count restriction was introduced.

## 2. Delivered Behavior

- `Program.created_at` converted to a KST calendar date is the signup-time
  source of truth for this promotion.
- The August cohort is identified by
  `price_guarantee_code=august_2026_lifetime` and
  `has_lifetime_price_guarantee=true`.
- Program saves, invoice creation, billing integrity checks, staff APIs, the
  developer console, and MRR aggregation all use each program's contract-price
  snapshot.
- A future standard list-price change can update non-August programs while the
  August cohort remains at KRW 145,000 supply and KRW 159,000 total.
- The public pricing experience presents an August special price, states that
  later new-signup pricing will increase, and guarantees no increase for
  August signups without inventing an unapproved future price or discount rate.
- Administrator and teacher billing screens display the lifetime-guarantee
  label for eligible programs.

## 3. Migration and Compatibility

- No price-policy schema or data migration was required. The existing
  `Program.created_at` and `monthly_price` fields are sufficient.
- Existing programs and issued invoices were not mutated by this release.
- The staff and platform-admin API additions are additive and optional for
  existing frontend consumers.
- The deployed integrated backend snapshot also contains the independently
  released core privacy-consent migration `0047`; it is unrelated to this
  price policy.
- Future standard-price migrations must explicitly exclude the August 2026
  guaranteed cohort.

## 6. Residual Risk

- The future standard signup price is intentionally unspecified. When the
  business approves that amount, the list-price constant, public copy, and a
  cohort-excluding rolling data migration must be released together.
- Promotion eligibility uses `Program.created_at`, matching the current
  one-program-per-tenant creation invariant. If signup onboarding is separated
  from program creation later, the eligibility source must be migrated to an
  explicit immutable signup timestamp before changing that invariant.
