## Full-suite findings closed after seal

Repeated full production E2E runs found problems that narrower release gates
did not expose:

- maintained-run cohort overlap allowed the account login throttle to affect
  controlled canaries;
- first-login guidance could cover clinic or homework controls;
- account recovery expected obsolete success copy despite the secure generic
  response;
- an interrupted OMR run could leave a score-edit draft that correctly
  disabled the OMR CTA and blocked ordinary lecture deletion;
- a newly acquired but unchanged score-edit lease also disabled the OMR CTA,
  even though there was no score change to protect;
- the controlled homework score writer had not acquired the now-required
  score-edit lease;
- the student grade-board check still required a removed section heading even
  when the current board visibly rendered three result links;
- the production residue canary did not yet enumerate timestamp-marked E2E
  lectures and sessions; and
- score cells for students not assigned to an exam or homework looked like
  ordinary empty cells and keyboard navigation could land on no-input cells.
