**Status:** Production-deployed / GO

**Scope:** Teacher-first Korean promotion copy, continuing August signup price,
mobile navigation repair, and frontend production closure

academy teachers and directors can understand without software terminology.

- Kept the main explanation factual: compare the real exam with material the
  academy prepared before the exam, then separately split question or concept

  with a presentation remote.

  The page does not render source documents again for each visitor.

- Added an explicit inner scroll region to the fixed mobile sidebar. The prior
  fixed sidebar had no vertical scroll container while the page body was
  locked, so the bottom actions could be clipped on short screens.
- States the price contract exactly: academies joining from 2026-08-01 through
  2026-08-31 keep the VAT-inclusive KRW 159,000 monthly price while they
  continue using the service. The normal monthly price remains KRW 198,000.
- Limited the automatic-grading claim to the implemented scope: some math
  short-answer questions whose answers are integers from 0 through 999.
- Aligned demo and consultation privacy disclosures with the exact required
  and optional fields collected by each form.
- Updated Terms to v1.2, effective 2026-07-26, to state that a continuing price
  explicitly promised by a separate agreement or signup promotion takes
  precedence.
