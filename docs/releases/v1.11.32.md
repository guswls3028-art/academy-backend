# V1.11.32 Release Notes

**Status:** Production-deployed / GO

**Release date:** 2026-07-28 KST

**Scope:** Friendly and state-safe bank-transfer collection guidance on the
academy billing page

## 1. Decision

GO for the customer-facing bank-transfer flow introduced in V1.11.26.

The production billing page now leads an academy owner through the real
sequence: select bank transfer, receive a payable invoice, verify its exact
amount and billing period, transfer to the operating account, and submit the
depositor name and transfer time. No account number is shown before a payable
invoice exists, and no paid or void historical invoice can be presented as a
new transfer target.

## 2. User Experience

- A three-step progress guide explains the collection flow in plain Korean.
- Changing from card billing to bank transfer requires an explicit
  confirmation and explains the lasting payment-mode change.
- The payable amount, supply amount, VAT, period, invoice number, and due date
  are shown before the account details.
- When there is no payable invoice, the page explicitly says not to transfer.
- A rejected notice restores the submitted depositor and transfer time and
  asks the user to correct the information without sending money again.
- Submitted and confirmed notices complete the three customer steps while the
  status panel communicates the operator-review state.
- Tax-invoice issuance failure has its own honest recovery message.
- Copy success, loading, retry, error, and owner-only states are accessible.
- The settings navigation becomes a full-width horizontal tab row on narrow
  screens instead of squeezing the payment form beside a fixed sidebar.

## 3. Financial and Contract Safety

- Only `PENDING` or `OVERDUE` invoices are rendered as payable.
- Historical `PAID`, `VOID`, or `FAILED` invoices are never used as an amount
  fallback.
- Account details remain hidden until the server has created or returned a
  payable invoice, closing the server's deposit-time validation gap.
- The canonical `AUTO_CARD` label is preserved as "카드 자동결제".
- Saved-card inspection and deletion remain available after switching to bank
  transfer, while new card registration is disabled in bank-transfer mode.
- The bank-transfer and saved-card mutations are rendered only for an owner or
  platform superuser, matching the server authorization boundary.
- Confirmation copy describes receipt posting rather than promising a
  subscription extension that the server may not perform for historical
  debt.

## 5. Audit Closure

The version-seal audit reported no critical findings. All confirmed high
findings were remediated before deployment:

- historical closed invoices no longer become payable targets;
- rejected notices no longer encourage a duplicate transfer;
- the card billing contract and saved-card control path remain truthful;
- account details cannot induce a transfer before invoice activation.

Medium findings for tax-invoice failure wording, owner authorization,
rejected-notice hydration, loading/retry accessibility, billing-period
display, neutral receipt wording, and paused/completed guide states were also
closed. No backend schema, migration, API, tenant-isolation, or deployment
infrastructure change was required.
