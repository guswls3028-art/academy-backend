## 6. Controlled Residuals

- JWT access and refresh tokens remain in `localStorage`; an HttpOnly refresh
  cookie requires a coordinated refresh/CSRF/session migration.
- Nova 2 Lite uses global inference. Users must mask unnecessary personal data
  or opt out of AI typing when Korea-only processing is required.
- Korean privacy counsel should review the final legal basis, notice timing,
  and processor-contract wording.
- The four-character minimum password remains an explicit product policy,
  mitigated by distributed throttling, hashing, short-lived access tokens,
  refresh rotation, and blacklisting.
- React Router's RSC-action advisory is unreachable in this React 18
  declarative SPA, and the remaining `brace-expansion` advisory is confined to
  a Node-side Excel packaging graph absent from the browser bundle and
  attacker-controlled server glob paths.
